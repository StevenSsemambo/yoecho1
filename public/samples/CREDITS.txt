Recorded instrument samples used by YoEcho (echo-music.js)

Taken from the tonejs-instruments library by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky (MIT License):
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments

Original recordings: Versilian Studios VSCO 2 Community Edition (piano, trumpet, trombone, flute, violin, ...),
Karoryfer Samples (electric bass, electric guitar, saxophone), University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios (acoustic guitar).
Files here were trimmed, level-matched and re-encoded as small mono mp3s.

- LinnDrum LM-2 kit: public domain, via oramics/sampled (https://github.com/oramics/sampled).

More recordings (v10):
- Electric piano, drawbar (Hammond-style) organ, clavinet, marimba, harp, upright bass and string ensemble come from the FluidR3 GM SoundFont,
  Copyright (c) 2000-2002, 2008, 2013 Frank Wen - MIT License, as converted to mp3 by the "soundfont-for-samplers" project (MIT).
- Pipe organ: VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0) via tonejs-instruments (MIT), as above.
- Extra percussion (cabasa, tambourine, cowbell, congas, clap, stick) from the public-domain LinnDrum LM-2 set; Roland CR-78 and TR-505 drum-machine kits
  are public domain; all via oramics/sampled (https://github.com/oramics/sampled).

Kalimba, glockenspiel (used for "bells") and choir "aahs" (v11): FluidR3 GM SoundFont, Copyright (c) 2000-2002, 2008, 2013 Frank Wen
(MIT; the midi-js-soundfonts conversion by Benjamin Gleitzman lists it under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 - credit given here in either case).

Voice "oohs" and synth choir (v18): FluidR3 GM SoundFont "Voice Oohs" and "Synth Choir", Copyright (c) 2000-2002, 2008, 2013 Frank Wen

The "vox", "hum" and synthetic fall-back voices are generated in the browser (no recordings).
