Recorded instrument samples used by YoEcho (echo-music.js)

Taken from the tonejs-instruments library by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky (MIT License):
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments

Original recordings: Versilian Studios VSCO 2 Community Edition (piano, trumpet, trombone, flute, violin, ...),
Karoryfer Samples (electric bass, electric guitar, saxophone), University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios (acoustic guitar).
Files here were trimmed, level-matched and re-encoded as small mono mp3s.
