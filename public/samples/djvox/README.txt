DJ voice clips (optional). Put short audio files you own or that are licensed for redistribution here (mp3, mono is fine),
then list them in manifest.json by category, e.g. {"drop":["airhorn-voice.mp3"],"tag":["tag1.mp3"],"hype":[],"adlib":[]}.
"drop" clips play on the big transition hits. Empty lists mean no voice, which is the default.
